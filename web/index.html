        Lock view

        Shows only the TV and channel buttons, plus Back, Home and Start over, on every device. This prevents accidental changes, but it isn't a security feature.

        The lock is on, and unlocked on this device until it reloads or you lock it again.

        Email alerts

        Sends an email when a channel button fails or no video starts, at most once per problem every 30 minutes. This can't report that this app, the computer running it or the internet is down.

        Use a separate Gmail account for this if you can. Turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an app password and enter it below, not your normal password.
